Discover more in our FAQs

What is the skin barrier?

Think of it as your skin’s personal bodyguard. The outermost layer of your skin is made of skin cells (like bricks) and lipids (like mortar). It locks in moisture, protects against pollution, bacteria and UV rays, and keeps skin balanced.

What are the signs of a damaged skin barrier?

Redness or inflammation, dry or flaky skin, itching or burning, breakouts or increased sensitivity, and skin that feels tight even after moisturising.

What causes a damaged skin barrier?

Over-exfoliation or harsh products, too many actives at once, environmental stress like sun, cold and pollution, poor diet or lack of hydration, and some skin conditions.

How do I repair and protect it?

Switch to gentle, hydrating cleansers, use a moisturiser rich in ceramides and fatty acids, avoid over-exfoliating, and always wear sunscreen during the day.

Glosskn suggests

Healthy skin starts with a strong barrier. Before chasing glow or anti-ageing, prioritise barrier health. It’s the foundation for all good skin.
