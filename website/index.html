Why it exists

Most makeup goes around the eyes. Most removers forget that.

i.

The eye area carries the most makeup

Liner, mascara, shadow and lashes all sit on the most sensitive skin of your face.

ii.

Gentle usually meant weak

Removers that were kind to the eyes left makeup behind. The ones that worked made them sting.

iii.

So she made one that does both

One face wash that removes your makeup, stays sting-free and tear-free, and feels comfortable on the eyes.

The founder

“If it stung even a little, we went back to the lab.”

Hi, I’m Jaspreet: makeup artist, educator and founder of GLOSSKN. I went on a mission to create something truly one of a kind.

  1. 12 years in makeup

    The most makeup goes around the eyes, yet nothing gentle and effective existed for that sensitive area.

  2. Multiple labs, many versions

    We kept testing and reworking the formula.

    V1V2V3V4

  3. Her own eyes first

    Every version went on my eyes first, then on friends and family.

  4. Until it truly felt gentle

    If it still stung, we weren’t done. That’s what Power Cleanse is about: gentle yet effective cleansing.

Jaspreet, founder of GLOSSKN
