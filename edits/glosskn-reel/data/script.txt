After 12 years in makeup, i stopped looking for a makeup remover and decided to create it. Hi, I am Jaspreet, makeup artist, educator and founder of glosskn. we see most amount of makeup goes around the eyes and yet there was no product available which was gentle and yet effective for the sensitive area. So I went on a mission to create something that was truly one of a kind..a facewash that could remove your makeup, be sting free, tear free and genuinely comfortable on the eyes. We worked with multiple labs and kept testing and reworking the formula. I tried every version on my eyes first and then with friends and family. If it stung even a little, we went back to the lab. We kept refining until it truly felt gentle. If it still stung, we were'nt done. That's what our power cleanse is all about: gentle yet effective cleansing.
